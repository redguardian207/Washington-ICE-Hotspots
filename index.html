        Find someone who may be detained

        ICE's Online Detainee Locator is the official search. It only shows people currently held by ICE, and it can take a day or two after an arrest for someone to appear.

        1. Search by A-Number (the 9-digit immigration case number) and country of birth. This is the most reliable way.
        2. Or search by first name, last name and country of birth. Spell the name the way it appears on official papers. Try other spellings and both last names if the person uses two.
        3. If nothing comes up, call the ICE Seattle Field Office (phone number on that page) or contact a legal-help group below. Most people arrested in this area are taken to the Northwest ICE Processing Center in Tacoma.

        Your car, license plate cameras and ICE

        What state law and state records say about plate and driver data reaching immigration agents.

        • License plate cameras. Senate Bill 6002, signed March 30, 2026 and in effect right away, bars police plate readers (often Flock cameras) from being used for immigration enforcement. They also cannot collect data near immigration or protected health care facilities, schools, courts, food banks or places of worship. Data must be deleted after 21 days unless it is evidence, and can only be shared in court proceedings. OPB
        • Driver and plate records. The governor's office says the state blocked 108,170 attempts by federal immigration agencies to search Washington driver and plate records from November 2025 to July 2026. ICE and Homeland Security Investigations lost access, and Customs and Border Protection access was suspended. OPB
        • Gaps remain. UW Center for Human Rights researchers report that federal agents have kept getting driver data through state systems, sometimes by searching again under a different agency code. UW Center for Human Rights
        • WSDOT traffic cameras are a separate system. They stream live pictures for traffic and are not plate readers.

        If you or someone you know needs help

        • ICE Online Detainee Locator: find out whether a person is held and where.
        • National Immigration Legal Services Directory: free or low-cost legal help by location.
        • We Have Rights: know-your-rights videos and guides in several languages.
        • NILC know-your-rights card: a printable card.
        • DHS Inspector General hotline: report misconduct by an ICE officer. You do not need the officer's name.
        • DHS Office for Civil Rights and Civil Liberties: file a civil rights complaint about how someone was treated.
        How this page works and what it leaves out
        Source
        The ICE List (theicelist.org) incident pages and community alerts for all of Washington, plus news reports found by web search. The ICE List gathers StopICE community alerts, eyewitness reports, court records and news. Legal-help links are on iceinmyarea.org/resources.
        Score
        Each report counts 1 on the day it happened and half that 90 days later, a quarter after 180 days, and so on. A town's score is the sum. It favors places with repeated and recent reports.
        Only with evidence
        Keeps reports backed by video, a court record, eyewitness detail matched to ICE's FOIA arrest log, or a news report. Drops unconfirmed community alerts and index listings.
        Areas
        Each town belongs to one of six regions: Seattle and King County; Tacoma and South Sound (Pierce, Thurston, Kitsap, Mason and the Olympic Peninsula); North Sound (Snohomish, Skagit, Whatcom, Island and San Juan); Southwest (Clark, Cowlitz, Lewis, the coast and the Gorge); Central and Tri-Cities (Yakima, Chelan, Grant, Kittitas, Okanogan, Benton and Franklin); and Spokane and Eastern. Community alerts that name only "Washington" with no town are left out. Bigger cities have more people and more reports, so compare towns with care.
        Road cameras
        Live images come straight from WSDOT's public camera feed (images.wsdot.wa.gov). This site does not save them. Cameras sometimes go offline for maintenance. When a report dated today or yesterday is added, an Active sighting box appears at the top with the nearest cameras. Nothing on this site scans the camera images for ICE; a sighting only appears from a report.
        Left out
        Reports that never reached The ICE List. Arrest totals in ICE's own data, which count people rather than places. Anything about future operations, which no public source here describes.
        Updates
        The ICE List is checked every hour, day and night, along with a news search, and new reports are added. The clock and "days ago" figures use the current time. Scores and the 90-day window count back from the most recent check.

        Report a sighting

        Reports are reviewed before anything is posted. A sighting goes on the site only after a second, independent source confirms it, such as a news story or another report of the same event.

        Do not include anyone's name, photo, license plate or social media handle. Describe the place and what happened.
